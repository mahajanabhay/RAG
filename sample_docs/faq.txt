Product: TaskFlow (project management SaaS)

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to Settings > Account > Reset Password. A reset link will be emailed within 5 minutes.

Q: What are the pricing plans?
A: Free plan supports up to 3 projects. Pro plan is $12/month with unlimited projects and priority support. Team plan is $29/month with 10 seats included.

Q: How do I invite team members?
A: Go to Settings > Team > Invite Member, enter their email, and assign a role (Admin, Editor, Viewer).

Q: Does TaskFlow have an API?
A: Yes, REST API access is available on Pro and Team plans. API keys can be generated under Settings > Developer.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your billing cycle.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Yes, TaskFlow is available on iOS and Android.
